# Executive Summary & Business Insights Report

## Project Title: HR Employee Attrition & Workforce Retention Analysis
**Author:** Data Analyst Portfolio Project  
**Domain:** Human Resources Analytics / Workforce Optimization  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Employee turnover poses significant direct and indirect costs, including recruitment expenses, lost productivity, and team burnout. This analysis evaluates workforce metrics from a sample of **1,470 employees** to identify primary drivers of attrition and recommend data-driven retention strategies.

### Key Headlines:
- **Baseline Attrition Rate:** **16.12%** (237 out of 1,470 employees attrited).
- **Primary Driver (Overtime):** Employees working regular overtime experience an attrition rate of **30.53%**, compared to **10.44%** for non-overtime staff (a **3x increase**).
- **Highest Attrition Department:** **Sales** leads with **20.63%** attrition, followed by **Human Resources (19.05%)** and **R&D (13.84%)**.
- **Tenure & Promotion Risk:** Employees who have not received a promotion in 3-5 years combined with low job satisfaction show the highest churn risk.

---

## 2. Key Analytical Findings

| Dimension | Insight / Metric | Key Business Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Overtime Work** | 30.53% Attrition Rate | Heavy workload causes severe burnout; 127 of 416 overtime workers left. |
| **Sales Department** | 20.63% Attrition Rate | Sales Executives & Representatives exhibit high mobility due to compensation structure. |
| **Age Demographic** | 25 - 34 Age Group | Early-career professionals have highest turnover seeking rapid career growth. |
| **Job Satisfaction** | Satisfaction Rating 1 & 2 | Employees with low satisfaction (<3) account for over 45% of total departures. |

---

## 3. Data-Driven Recommendations

1. **Overtime & Workload Balancing:**
   - Cap consecutive overtime hours per employee.
   - Implement workload audits in R&D and Sales to prevent employee burnout.

2. **Sales Compensation & Incentive Restructuring:**
   - Re-evaluate commission tiers and career growth tracks for Sales Executives to improve 2-year retention.

3. **Early Career Mentorship (Ages 25–34):**
   - Introduce structured 18-month career progression paths for junior analysts and technicians.

4. **Proactive Retention Risk Score Model:**
   - Operationalize the Python / SQL Risk Score Model to alert HR managers when an employee reaches "High Risk" criteria before resignation.
